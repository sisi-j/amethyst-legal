Free Discord apps for DonutSMP players

Know who you're dealing with.

Link Minecraft accounts, share builds, and check a server's record before you trade. One account and one library work across every server that uses them.

Donut Verify

Prove who you are in-game

  • Members link their DonutSMP account once, and it counts in every server that uses Donut Verify.
  • Two ways in: DonutSMP's official Discord link, with no need to be online, or a small in-game payment.
  • Your verified role is handed out automatically.
  • Right-click any member → Apps → Minecraft account to see who they are in-game, with live stats.

Once it's added, run /verify-settings role and keep Donut Verify's own role above the one it gives out.

Asks for: Manage Roles, to give the verified role. Send Messages and Embed Links, to post a Verify button and a welcome.

Global Schematics

A shared library of builds

  • Browse and search builds from every server, each with a textured 3D preview.
  • Share your own .schem or .litematic. Our team checks every build before it's listed.
  • Download a build, then rate it from 1 to 5 stars.
  • Follow a category to get a DM when something new lands, or have new builds posted in a channel.

Works straight away. Try /schematic browse.

Asks for: Send Messages, Embed Links, Attach Files and Read Message History, to post new builds with their preview and a welcome.

Donut Server Trust

Check a server before you trade

  • See how a server is rated by members who've been there at least a week.
  • Report a scam. Our team reviews every report before it counts against a server.
  • Verified partner servers are marked, and a leaderboard shows the best-rated.
  • Add it to your own account to check any server from anywhere, even where it isn't installed.

Works straight away. Try /trust check.

Asks for: Send Messages and Embed Links, for a welcome post. That's all.

Getting started

  1. Add an app

    Pick a server you manage. Discord shows exactly what the app asks for before anything happens.

  2. Say hello

    It posts a short getting-started message. Type /help any time for every command, each one clickable.

  3. Share it

    Every /help has an "Add to your server" button, so members can bring the apps to their own servers.

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. All three apps are free, with no premium tier and nothing locked behind a payment.

Is this made by DonutSMP?

No. Amethyst is an independent community project, not affiliated with DonutSMP, Mojang Studios, Microsoft or Discord.

What do you store about me?

Only what each feature needs, such as your linked Minecraft name. The Privacy Policy lists all of it, and /unlink removes your link everywhere.

Found a problem?

Open an issue. Issues are public, so please leave out personal details.
